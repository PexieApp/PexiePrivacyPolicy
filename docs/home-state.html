Home State

Your whole day, at a glance.

Home State turns your iPad into an always-on dashboard. Put your calendar, weather, unread mail, local news, reminders, photos and HomeKit scenes on one board, and arrange it the way you like.

Requires an iPad with iPadOS 26 or later.

Help

How do I add my mail?

Open Settings (the gear) › Mail Accounts › Add Account, then sign in with an app-specific password from your mail provider, not your regular password. Home State only reads your inbox, so messages stay unread.

Why is the Scenes card empty?

The Scenes card shows the scenes from the Home app. Set up a home and a few scenes there, then allow Home access when Home State asks.

How do I stop the screen from staying on?

Home State keeps the screen awake by default so it can sit on a desk or wall. Turn it off in Settings › Display › Keep Screen Awake.

How do I rearrange the board?

Tap the grid button at the top right, or press and hold any card. Drag cards to move them, and use the ⋯ button to change a card's size or settings.
