Home State Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Home State does not collect, sell or share your personal data. The developer runs no servers that receive information from the app, and the app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.

Home State shows your own information, such as your calendar, reminders and photos, on your iPad. It reads that information on your device, with your permission, and keeps it there. A few features contact other services to work, such as Apple Weather for the forecast. This policy explains each one.

Information Home State accesses on your iPad

Home State asks for each permission only when you turn on the feature that needs it. You can skip any of them, and you can change them anytime in the iPad Settings app under Home State.

PermissionWhat Home State does with it
CalendarsShows your upcoming events on the Calendar and Countdown widgets.
RemindersShows your incomplete reminders on the Reminders widget.
Location (while using the app)Finds the weather and local news for where you are. See the next sections for what is sent.
PhotosShows photos from the album you choose in the Photos widget.
Home (HomeKit)Lists your HomeKit scenes and runs the one you tap.

This information is read on your iPad only to display it. Home State does not copy your calendar, reminders, photos or home data off your device, and does not change them, except running a HomeKit scene when you tap it.

Mail

If you add a mail account, Home State connects directly from your iPad to your mail provider's server, such as iCloud, Gmail or QQ Mail, using the IMAP protocol over an encrypted connection. No other server sits in between.

  • What it reads: the sender, subject and date of unread messages in your inbox, plus the number of unread messages. It does not download message bodies or attachments.
  • Read-only: Home State opens your inbox in read-only mode, so it never marks messages as read, moves or deletes them, or sends mail.
  • Your password: stored in your iPad's keychain, available only on this device. Your server address and username are stored in the app's data on your iPad.
  • Message details: kept in memory only while the app runs, and never saved or sent anywhere else.

Services Home State contacts

Some features need information from the internet. Home State sends only what each request needs, directly from your iPad. Like any internet request, each service can see your device's IP address. These services handle that information under their own privacy policies.

ServiceWhenWhat is sent

Apple MapsLocation is allowed, or you open a photo that has a locationYour location, or the photo's, to find the place name shown
Google NewsLocal News is on and location is allowedYour city's name, as a news search
News feeds you addYou add an RSS or Atom feedA request for that feed, to the site that publishes it

You can turn off Local News in Settings, under News Feeds, so your city is never sent to Google. Turning off location access for Home State in the Settings app stops your location from being sent to Apple.

Where your data is stored and how to delete it

Everything Home State saves stays on your iPad: your board layout, widget settings, news feeds, display preferences and mail account settings. It is not synced to iCloud or uploaded anywhere.

  • Remove a mail account: Settings › Mail Accounts, then swipe the account to delete it. This also deletes its password from the keychain.
  • Remove a news feed: Settings › News Feeds, then swipe the feed to delete it.
  • Delete everything: delete the Home State app. To be sure saved mail passwords are gone too, remove your mail accounts first, since iPadOS can keep keychain items after an app is deleted.
  • Revoke access: turn off Calendars, Reminders, Location, Photos or Home for Home State in the iPad Settings app.

Children

Home State is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If Home State changes how it handles information, this policy will be updated and the date at the top will change. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
